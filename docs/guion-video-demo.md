# Guion — Video demo de 3 minutos (Loom)

**Setup antes de grabar:** dos ventanas lado a lado — izquierda el juego
(/play), derecha el Manager Dashboard (/manager). Teléfono a la mano con
/demo abierto (o segunda pestaña). Corre "🧹 Reset" y luego "🌱 Sembrar día
de demo" para que el floor tenga vida. Audio del sistema ACTIVADO (las
alarmas son parte del pitch). Habla en inglés si el video es para dealers.

---

## Escena 1 — El gancho (0:00 – 0:25)

*Pantalla: el juego, tu avatar parado en el sales floor.*

> "This is Race to the Sale. It's not a dashboard on top of your CRM —
> it's a game your salespeople actually live in. Every lead, every call,
> every appointment happens HERE. Watch what happens when a real lead
> comes in."

## Escena 2 — El lead race (0:25 – 1:10)

*Desde el teléfono: inyecta un lead de CarGurus. La alarma suena.*

> "A CarGurus lead just hit the floor. Every rep sees it, every rep hears
> it. The clock is running — industry standard says respond in 5 minutes,
> top stores do it in under 1."

*Corre el avatar al Lead Board, SPACE, sale el toast "⚡ LIGHTNING RESPONSE
+75 XP".*

> "First to grab it owns it. Respond in under 30 seconds and you get the
> maximum XP. Slow responses earn less. Speed is the whole game — because
> speed is what sells cars."

## Escena 3 — El robo (1:10 – 1:50)

*Desde el teléfono: "😈 Abrir leads sin trabajar para robo". Pulso naranja.*

> "Here's the rule that changes behavior: claim a lead and sit on it for
> 20 minutes without working it? It goes up for grabs — and your teammate
> can STEAL it, points and all. Nobody lets a lead die in this store."

*Si tienes segunda cuenta: muestra el robo y el toast 💀. Si no, muestra la
línea naranja "😈 STEAL" en el board y el toast de advertencia.*

## Escena 4 — El trabajo real (1:50 – 2:20)

*Camina al Computer Desk, manda un email real con template. Luego tecla M,
marca una cita 📅.*

> "And this isn't pretend work. That email? It actually sent — from your
> store's domain, logged to your CRM. Appointments, sales, everything the
> rep does feeds their score. No double data entry. The game IS the
> workflow."

## Escena 5 — La pantalla del manager (2:20 – 2:50)

*Cambia a la ventana del Manager Dashboard.*

> "Meanwhile, this is YOUR view. Untouched leads turn red at 5 minutes.
> Every rep's activities against the 90-a-day standard. Appointments,
> sales, average response time, leads lost to steals. You walk the floor
> without leaving your office."

## Escena 6 — Cierre (2:50 – 3:10)

*Vuelve al juego, leaderboard visible.*

> "Internet leads close at 10% across the industry. The stores that
> respond fastest close at 15 to 20. Race to the Sale exists to move that
> number. Want to see it live on your floor? Let's set up a pilot."
